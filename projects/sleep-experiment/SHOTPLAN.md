# Shot plan: `sleep-experiment` (1080x1920, 30 fps)

> **Superseded by v3 (2026-11-05):** the delivered cut has 23 one-action shots (the list is in `src/videos/sleep-experiment/story.ts`, `SH`), a dread look, a new hook, and gestures for every line. The table below is the approved v1 plan, kept for reference.

Voice: 74.0 s. Film total **2280 f (76 s)**, which leaves a 1.5 s hold after "tight".
There are 18 shots, averaging 4.2 s. Cuts land on sentence boundaries; where one sentence carries two subjects, a motivated camera move links them instead of a cut.
**Horror rule:** gore never appears on screen. Every "payoff" is a reaction, a prop or a light change. The soldiers' faces carry what's behind the door.
Since this is horror, the "gag" column holds the scare or payoff beat. There are no jokes.

## Places (all new, built as neutral library sets with a `variant`)
- **chamber**: a sealed concrete test room. Two porthole windows, a steel door with a wheel, 5 bare cots, a shelf of books, a floor drain, 2 ceiling gas vents, wall microphone grilles, a wall calendar and a clock. Light: `storm` with a sickly green grade and gas haze.
- **controlRoom**: the observation side. A steel desk, an intercom mic and speaker, an O2 dial, headphones, a porthole into the chamber and a calendar. Light: `dusk` under amber bulbs.
- **bedroom** (finale): a kid's bed, a nightstand lamp and a moonlit window. Light: `dusk` turning to dark blue.

Variety: all three sets are new. The last videos used day/golden light; this one is storm/dusk only, with a new green-teal + amber palette and no music.

## Cast and props
- **New, into the library:** `PRISONER` outfit (grey uniform, shaved head; a "gaunt" option with eye bags), `SOVIET_SOLDIER` (ushanka, rifle) and `COMMANDER` (peaked cap, greatcoat).
- **Reused:** `SCIENTIST` (with a dark-haired variant).
- **New props:** gas vent, wall mic grille, O2 dial, intercom, pistol, tin-can stack, steel door wheel, nightstand lamp.
- **Text props (story details, not facts):** calendar pages `DAY 1..5`, `DAY 9`, `DAY 14`, and a `30 DAYS` card.

## Sound (SFX only, no music)
- **Beds:** low chamber hum and drip under every scene; gas hiss in shots 1-2 and 12; a quiet bedroom clock tick at the end.
- **Hits:**
  - valve squeak, calendar page flips, whisper textures (filtered noise, no words), clock-hand ticks spinning;
  - the scream as a muffled pounding plus a rising strained tone (no synthetic human scream), cut dead at "tore";
  - paper slaps, headphone taps, rifle clacks, the cigarette landing;
  - the door wheel clank and creak, a boom on "screamed", water swirl;
  - the door clang, one gunshot, the cap rolling;
  - bulb buzz and flicker, the lamp click.
- **Silences:** after "went silent" (875-948) and after "tight".
