## Description
Every morning, Dave steps into a booth, gets scanned… and erased. A copy walks out in Tokyo.

Real physicists already teleport things, but only the *state* of a particle, and the original is always destroyed. Here is the bill for doing it to a whole human: the atoms, the data, the energy, the "is that still you?" problem, and what happens to cities and borders if it ever works.
