## Description
Five prisoners. A sealed room. A gas that makes sleep impossible. Stay awake for thirty days and you walk free... 😶

⚠️ This is FICTION. "The Russian Sleep Experiment" is a famous internet horror story (a "creepypasta"), not a real Soviet experiment. No records of it exist. We retold it in our own words as an animated short.
